Introduction

Water Street at Wellington Stream, located in a densely populated area, has become a hotspot for environmental degradation due to poor waste management practices. Residents and businesses in the area frequently dump their household and commercial waste into the stream, contributing to serious environmental and health issues. The improper disposal of garbage has led to clogged waterways, preventing the free flow of water and exacerbating the risk of flooding during the rainy season.

Problem Statement

The Wellington Stream, a crucial natural watercourse, has become severely obstructed due to the indiscriminate dumping of solid waste. During the rainy season, the accumulation of waste in the stream causes a serious blockage, leading to severe flooding. This flooding has caused significant damage to properties, disrupted livelihoods, and posed a threat to public health. The water from the stream often flows into people's homes, destroying personal belongings, infrastructure, and crops, leading to economic hardships.

Causes of the Problem

Irresponsible Waste Disposal

The habitual dumping of trash by residents and local businesses who view the stream as a convenient waste disposal area, blocking the water flow.

Infrastructure & Urbanization

A severe lack of sufficient waste disposal systems and inadequate garbage collection services, paired with rapid urbanization and overcrowding.

Climate Change

Changing weather patterns and heavy rainfall during the rainy season worsen the problem when combined with existing waste blockages.

Impact of the Problem

Property Damage

Floodwaters and waste materials overflow into homes, ruining furniture, electrical appliances, and household items, leading to costly repairs.

Health Hazards

Stagnant water filled with garbage creates breeding grounds for mosquitoes and bacteria, risking outbreaks of cholera and malaria.

Loss of Livelihood

Flooding inundates shops, markets, and commercial establishments, causing heavy financial losses in the informal business sector.

Environmental Degradation

Accumulated waste impacts local aquatic life, drastically reduces water quality, and harms the local ecosystem's biodiversity.
