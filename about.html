Project Overview

Project Title: Tackling Flooding and Pollution in Water Street at Wellington Stream

Project Lead: Lamin Bangura

Implementation Plan

This phased approach ensures that both immediate relief and long-term environmental systems are successfully put in place.

Phase Timeline Key Actions & Focus Areas
Phase 1: Awareness & Education 1 - 3 Months Launch public education campaigns and set up interactive community meetings/workshops.
Phase 2: Infrastructure & Rehab 3 - 6 Months Begin setting up the waste collection systems, initiate stream cleaning, and implement flood prevention measures.
Phase 3: Long-Term Solutions 6 - 12 Months Develop robust waste infrastructure, continue scheduled clean-up activities, and maintain newly installed systems.

Conclusion

The problem of flooding and pollution in Water Street at Wellington Stream is a pressing concern that requires immediate action. Through community-based waste management, stream rehabilitation, infrastructure improvements, and stronger policy enforcement, the situation can be mitigated. By involving the community, government, and NGOs in the process, we can create a sustainable and flood-resilient environment for the residents of Wellington Street.
