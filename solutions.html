Proposed Solutions

A multi-faceted strategy to mitigate flooding and manage waste sustainably.

A. Community-Based Waste Management

Awareness Campaigns

Organize public education programs and community meetings. Teach proper waste segregation and partner with local schools and youth organizations.

Collection Points

Set up designated waste collection points along Water Street and near the stream to structure trash disposal properly. Ensure regular collections.

Waste Segregation

Encourage separation of organic waste from non-biodegradable materials to maximize management efficiency.

B. Stream Rehabilitation

Regular Cleaning

Launch periodic cleaning initiatives with volunteers, NGOs, and municipal authorities. Use mechanical equipment like pumps to clear large blockages.

Flood Barriers & Channels

Construct temporary or permanent flood barriers to protect homes, and build diversion channels to direct excess water away safely.

C. Infrastructure Development

Improved Drainage Systems

Upgrade drainage systems to handle larger volumes of water and ensure consistent maintenance of existing sewers.

Waste Disposal Facilities

Invest in waste sorting facilities, recycling stations, and composting units while providing larger household bins.

D. Policy & Collaboration

Policy & Regulatory Measures

Enforce strict anti-dumping regulations and fines. Introduce incentive programs like neighborhood prizes for clean spaces.

Community & NGO Engagement

Organize monthly volunteer clean-up days. Partner with government agencies for funding and tools, and empower local leadership.
